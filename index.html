About POLISHED

POLISHED is a specialist branding studio crafting premium, "quiet luxury" visual identities exclusively for e-commerce skincare storefronts. We blend world-class minimalism with a distinctive "Premium Bengali" sensibility — combining local cultural resonance with international old-money refinement — to turn passive scrollers into loyal buyers.

Core Services

  • E-commerce Visual Strategy — Trust-building storefront assets engineered for conversion.
  • High-Conversion Social Media Design — Scroll-stopping creative that drives qualified buyers.
  • Premium Brand Identity Systems — Logo, typography, color, and bespoke visual language.
  • Skincare Packaging Design — Refined, shelf-ready packaging for premium product lines.
  • UI/UX Design — Storefront and landing page design tailored to skincare commerce.

Selected Work

Featured engagements include conversion-focused branding for NIVEA Soft and additional skincare visual systems delivered under NDA with top-tier brands. Comprehensive case studies are available on request.

The POLISHED Process

  1. Discovery & Strategy — Deep analysis of brand positioning and consumer psychology.
  2. Visual Architecture — Crafting the quiet-luxury identity system.
  3. Execution & Launch — Delivery of storefront-ready assets and ongoing visual stewardship.

Design Philosophy

Premium, minimalist, and deeply trustworthy. English typography is set in Cormorant Garamond; Bengali in Noto Serif Bengali. The aesthetic is timeless, editorial, and engineered for long-term brand authority — not short-term trends.

Investment Structure

POLISHED operates on a bespoke, high-ROI investment model rather than fixed packages. Engagements are scoped to the brand's stage, market, and ambition. Comprehensive identity systems typically span 3 to 6 weeks.
